# AI Governance Contract

## Phase 9B: Controlled AI Orchestration Governance

### AI Allowed To
✅ **Interpret requests** (Natural language parsing)
✅ **Extract parameters** (Scenario planning)
✅ **Fill schemas** (Typed schema framework)
✅ **Select evidence** (Evidence selection engine)
✅ **Explain outcomes** (Decision narrator)
✅ **Generate narratives** (Decision narrator)
✅ **Answer user questions** (Natural language interaction)

### AI Forbidden From
❌ **Revenue calculations** (Belongs to Phase 5 Impact Engine)
❌ **Delay calculations** (Belongs to Phase 5 Impact Engine)
❌ **SLA calculations** (Belongs to Phase 5 Impact Engine)
❌ **Simulation outcomes** (Belongs to Phase 6 Simulation Engine)
❌ **Intervention rankings** (Belongs to Phase 7 Optimization Engine)
❌ **Constraint evaluations** (Belongs to Phase 7 Optimization Engine)
❌ **Optimization scores** (Belongs to Phase 7 Optimization Engine)

### Governance Rule
**Every numerical claim must originate from deterministic outputs**
- Revenue figures → Phase 5 Impact Engine
- Delay calculations → Phase 5 Impact Engine
- SLA breaches → Phase 5 Impact Engine
- Recovery decisions → Phase 7 Optimization Engine
- Optimization scores → Phase 7 Optimization Engine
- Simulation outcomes → Phase 6 Simulation Engine
- Evidence support → Phase 8 Evidence Layer

### Core Principle
**The AI is allowed to explain business truth, but it is never allowed to create business truth.**
