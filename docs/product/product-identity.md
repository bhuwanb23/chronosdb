# ChronosDB Product Identity

## Locked identity

- **Name:** ChronosDB
- **Title:** ChronosDB: Auditable Counterfactual Intelligence for
  Supply-Chain Disruptions
- **Category:** Db2-backed operational decision support
- **Tagline:** Reconstruct the past. Simulate the future. Prove the decision.

## One-sentence description

ChronosDB helps supply-chain operations managers understand the cascading
impact of a supplier disruption, compare feasible recovery interventions, and
verify recommendations against evidence stored in IBM Db2.

## Product promise

For a critical supplier outage, ChronosDB answers:

1. What happened?
2. What will be affected?
3. What happens if no action is taken?
4. Which recovery interventions are feasible?
5. Why is one intervention preferable?

## Differentiator

ChronosDB is a deterministic scenario-analysis system with a possible
AI-assisted interface. It is not a chatbot that independently calculates or
invents operational outcomes.

## Capability boundaries

ChronosDB may:

- reconstruct a defined operational state;
- calculate a documented impact cascade;
- compare constrained recovery scenarios;
- preserve evidence and calculation traces;
- explain verified results for human review.

ChronosDB does not currently:

- execute procurement or production actions;
- claim to predict the real world;
- replace an ERP system;
- guarantee an optimal business decision;
- use unsupported numbers in explanations.

## Canonical language

Use **projects counterfactual outcomes**, not “predicts the future.” Use
**revenue at risk**, not “revenue lost,” unless a later rule explicitly proves
loss. Use **recommendation for human review**, not “autonomous decision.”
