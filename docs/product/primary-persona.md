# Primary Persona

## Ananya Rao — synthetic persona

Ananya is a regional supply-chain operations manager at a mid-sized
electronics manufacturer. This persona is synthetic and is used only to make
requirements concrete.

### Operating context

- Two production facilities
- Three warehouses
- Multiple approved component suppliers
- Hundreds of open customer orders
- A critical microcontroller supplier has reported a 14-day outage

### Responsibilities

- protect production continuity;
- coordinate procurement, production, and warehouse teams;
- protect customer service commitments;
- control recovery expenditure;
- explain decisions to leadership;
- preserve a reviewable record.

### Current process

Ananya combines ERP exports, spreadsheets, supplier calls, and manual impact
analysis. A complex disruption can take two to six hours to compare safely.

### Needs

Ananya needs a fast first comparison that is:

- based on the same operational state for every option;
- explicit about constraints and infeasible actions;
- clear about affected orders and service exposure;
- traceable to source records and calculation rules;
- usable without trusting unsupported AI claims.

### Desired outcome

Within five minutes of entering a valid disruption, Ananya can review a
defensible baseline and compare recovery options. Human approval remains
required.
