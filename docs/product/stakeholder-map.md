# Stakeholder Map

## Primary user

### Supply-chain operations manager

Owns the disruption response, coordinates teams, balances service and cost,
and explains the selected recovery plan.

Primary decisions:

- which orders are at risk;
- which bottleneck requires attention;
- which feasible intervention should be reviewed first;
- whether the evidence is sufficient to act.

## Secondary stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Main question |
|---|---|
| Production planner | Which production orders and capacities are affected? |
| Procurement analyst | Which alternate supply or expedition options are approved? |
| Warehouse manager | What inventory can move without violating safety stock? |
| Customer-service manager | Which customer commitments may breach? |
| Finance or business manager | What cost and revenue exposure is involved? |
| Auditor or risk reviewer | Can the decision be replayed and verified? |

## Priority when needs conflict

1. Data integrity and intervention feasibility
2. Customer service commitments
3. Production continuity
4. Revenue protection
5. Recovery cost
6. Narrative convenience

This is a default product priority. Future optimization settings must make
business weights and constraints visible rather than hiding them in prose.

## Stakeholder boundaries

Stakeholders may review, challenge, and approve a recommendation. ChronosDB
does not directly contact suppliers, move inventory, change production orders,
or notify customers.
