# Phase 3J–3K: Scenario and Evidence Model

Scenario inputs contain disruptions, hard constraints, and isolated candidate
parameters. They do not contain calculated recommendations or expected output
labels. Supplier outage is the mandatory MVP event; other event categories are
controlled benchmark coverage.

The evidence corpus contains 175 project-authored documents: supplier notices,
incident reports, procedures, policies, and recovery summaries. Metadata carries
effective dates, status, supplier/component references, confidentiality, and
source type. Documents explain policy and context only; operational facts and
ground truth come from structured data and the independent oracle.
