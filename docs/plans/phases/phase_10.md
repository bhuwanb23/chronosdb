The defining principle of Phase 10

ChronosDB does not ask users to trust an AI recommendation. It preserves enough evidence, controls, lineage, and audit history that the recommendation can be independently verified long after it was made.

This phase completes the trust foundation of the system and creates the strongest enterprise-grade differentiator in the entire ChronosDB architecture before moving into Phase 11: Backend and API Integration.
