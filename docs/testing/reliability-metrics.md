### Metric Definitions
- **Critical Workflow Success Rate:** Percentage of successful executions across all scenarios
- **Recovery Success:** Percentage of successful recoveries after failures
- **Replay Accuracy:** Percentage of replays matching original results
- **Determinism:** Percentage of executions producing identical results
- **Defect Escape Rate:** Percentage of S1 defects escaping to production
