# ChronosDB — Music and Sound Brief

- **Mood:** calm focus that builds to a resolved, confident close. Think "decision made", not "hype".
- **Tempo:** 90–100 BPM, no vocals.
- **Structure:** soft start under the opening pain line (0:00–0:20); gentle lift at the cascade and futures beats; a single clear resolution cue at the INT-GOLD-C recommendation (~1:50); quiet bed under evidence and replay; closing hit on the memorable line (2:45).
- **Mix:** voice-over sits 8–12 dB above music; music ducks during narration; no music under the final line.
- **Sound effects (sparingly):** soft tick on each click, a subtle "lock" on the recommendation card, a soft chime on the replay confirmation.
- **Avoid:** stock "tech montage" beats, dramatic drops, anything that competes with the voice-over.
